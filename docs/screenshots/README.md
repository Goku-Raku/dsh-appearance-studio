The welcome screens show light/dark gradients. The two conversation images
illustrate **independent frame and conversation blur and transparency (tint
strength)**: one uses a blurred frame with a sharp conversation image, the other
a sharp frame with a blurred conversation image. These are example combinations;
mode and other settings also differ. No precise slider values are inferred.

The frame comprises the top bar and sidebar. Tint transparency is internal to
the app; it does not expose other OS windows. Screenshots containing the bundled
fan artwork inherit the [separate image terms](../../assets/README.md). Host logos
and interface elements remain the property of their respective rights holders.
